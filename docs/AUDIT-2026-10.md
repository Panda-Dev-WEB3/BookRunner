## M-1: held marks settle nothing

**Finding.** The mark service keeps signing marks while the oracle holds the underlying's price (session
closed), valuing the book at the held Friday price. A Junior holder whose notice fits inside the closed
session (devnet notice 15 min; the charter had a maximum notice, no minimum) redeemed at the pre-gap value
and the reopen gap fell on the holders who stayed. PoC: the sponsor's Junior lost 60% instead of 20%; the
early exiter avoided a 4k loss.

**Fix.**

- `BRTypes.MarkInput` gains `bool held`, signed: `MARK_TYPEHASH = keccak256("Mark(uint256 bookId,uint64
  periodEnd,uint256 navUsd,uint256 deployedValueUsd,uint64 flowNonce,bytes32 inventoryRoot,bytes32
  pnlJsonHash,bytes32 receiptsRoot,bool held)")`, mirrored in `packages/shared/src/eip712.ts` (`markTypes`)
  with a fixed struct-hash vector checked on both sides. A relayer cannot flip the flag.
- The mark service (`services/mark/src/domain/held.ts`) sets `held` from the oracle's **signed** price state
  (`PriceUpdate.held`, the signed bundle price when it is newer than the stored one, else the stored one) of
  every price the valuation used: the book's underlying and each desk Stock Token position held. The flag is
  also recorded in the published MarkPnl statement (`pnl.held`, hashed into `pnlJsonHash`).
- `Book.applyMark` on a held mark applies the P&L (waterfall, backstop, drawdown kill, share prices) but
  settles no redemption bucket and no top-up round (no `Tranche.settleAtMark` call, so `lastSettledIndex`
  does not move). The first non-held mark settles every bucket due by then, and a due round, at its post-P&L
  prices, so the reopen gap is shared pro rata by everyone in the book when the session closed. Emits
  `MarkHeld(bookId, markId, heldSince, settled)`.
- **No starvation.** `Book.heldSince()` is the periodEnd of the first held mark of the current run (reset by
  any non-held mark). Once `periodEnd - heldSince >= BookLogic.MAX_HELD_DEFERRAL` (7 days) held marks settle
  again, so a long hold (suspension, delisting) defers exits but never blocks them. 7 days covers every
  scheduled closed session (a weekend plus a holiday is at most about 4 days) with margin. Past it the
  valuation is the held price again, which is the pre-fix behaviour for that rare case; Task 7 adds a
  timelocked settle-at-price path for permanently held markets. `requestRedeem`, claims and every other exit
  stay ungated (the M-1 tests cover requests during a hold).
- **Defence in depth: minimum Junior notice.** `MarketCharter.validate` returns `BAD_NOTICE` when
  `juniorNoticeSeconds < config.markInterval()` (or, as before, above 30 days), mirrored in
  `services/charter` and `services/api` validation (when the interval is known), the web form default (1 day)
  and `scripts/launch-devnet.ts` (notice raised to the interval). With at least one interval of notice a
  Junior request always bears at least one full mark period of P&L before its bucket settles. The auditor's
  alternative (notice >= the longest closed session plus one interval) was not taken: it would impose a
  multi-day minimum notice on every book, and held marks already remove the closed-session exit; the minimum
  is a backstop for a mark wrongly signed as not held, not the primary fix.
- **Senior** (no notice) is covered by the same deferral: a Senior request during a hold settles at the first
  non-held mark.

**Residual.** A hold that outlasts 7 days settles at the held valuation (bounded deferral was chosen over
blocking exits). If the oracle's price state is unreadable for the underlying the service logs it and does not
flag the mark held for it. An Orderly book's venue equity comes from ops-venue's signed report; its mark is
flagged held from the underlying's oracle state like an engine book's (the venue's own off-hours marking is not
a separate input).
